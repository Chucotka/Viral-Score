Viral Score

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 14, 2026

Viral Score is a content analysis app for posts, videos, and links. This policy explains what data we may process and why.

Information we may process

  • Telegram user ID and bot session data
  • Content you submit for analysis, such as video files, links, captions, transcripts, and prompts
  • Analysis history and usage counts
  • Payment and access status

How we use it

  • To analyze viral potential and generate recommendations
  • To unlock paid access and manage quotas
  • To restore recent analyses and improve the app experience

Payments

Payments may be processed through Telegram Stars or an external checkout provider. Payment handling is performed by Telegram or the selected provider, depending on the payment path used.

Data retention

We keep analysis history and access state only as long as needed for the product experience and account access. You can request deletion by contacting the bot owner.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We only share data with service providers that are required to operate the app, such as Telegram and the configured analysis backend.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the bot owner or support channel linked from the app.
